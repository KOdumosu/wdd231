Non-Profit Membership

Cost: Free

Validity: 12 months

Renewal: Annually


Overview

The Non-Profit (NP) Membership is designed for registered non-profit organizations, community groups, charities, and associations that support local development but operate with limited funding.


Benefits:

•Basic listing in the organization’s online directory

•Access to newsletters and important announcements

•Invitations to selected community events and workshops

•Opportunity to participate in outreach and advocacy programs

•Networking opportunities with businesses and other non-profits

•Ideal for: Non-profit organizations seeking visibility, connection, and community engagement without financial commitment.

Bronze Membership

Cost: ₦30,000 per year

Validity: 12 months

Renewal: Annually


Overview

The Bronze Membership offers essential benefits for small businesses and startups looking to establish their presence and connect within the business community.

Benefits:

•Standard business listing in the online directory

•Access to member-only newsletters and updates

•Invitations to networking events and training sessions

•Eligibility to attend business seminars and workshops

•Use of the membership badge on business materials

•Ideal for: Startups and small businesses that want basic exposure and networking opportunities.

Silver Membership

Cost: ₦55,000 per year

Validity: 12 months

Renewal: Annually


Overview

The Silver Membership provides enhanced visibility and added benefits for growing businesses that want stronger engagement and promotional opportunities.


Benefits:

•Featured business listing in the online directory

•Priority access to networking events and workshops

•Discounts on event registrations and training programs

•Opportunities to collaborate on community projects

•Business promotion through selected digital platforms

•Access to business development resources and support

Ideal for: Established businesses seeking increased exposure, growth support, and stronger professional connections.

Gold Membership

Cost: ₦80,000 per year

Validity: 12 months

Renewal: Annually

Overview

The Gold Membership is a premium option offering maximum visibility, influence, and exclusive access to high-value opportunities within the organization.


Benefits:

•Premium featured listing at the top of the online directory

•Priority sponsorship and advertising opportunities

•Exclusive invitations to executive and VIP events

•Recognition as a key supporter on the website and at events

•First access to partnership and collaboration opportunities

•Personalized business support and consultation opportunities

•Greater influence in organizational programs and initiatives

Ideal for: Large businesses, corporate organizations, and sponsors seeking top-tier visibility and leadership presence.
